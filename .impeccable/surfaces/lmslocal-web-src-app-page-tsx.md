# Landing page (lmslocal-web `/`)

Mode: Persuade. Two equal doors: players into the free national game (every account is in), and organisers starting a fundraiser competition for their club, pub or workplace. The national game is not built yet, so its figures and actions are mocks: figures carry a visible SAMPLE tag, and the actions route to sign-up and the dashboard.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the Saturday results service. Scores type in, and each one knocks thousands out of a national game. It refuses the category's split hero with a phone mock-up and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Phosphor-black screen. Broadcast-text colours with fixed roles: yellow is the national game and the primary action, cyan is organisers and clubs, green is through, red is out, white is text, blue is neutral page banners. VT323 caps for display and the feed, with double-height headings. Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for body copy. Page-number banners, an index and a four-key coloured fastext bar for navigation, and dotted-leader ledgers for figures.

STORY: A visitor sees a live round knocking people out and understands they're already in a free national game the moment they sign up. An organiser sees the same machine run their club's fundraiser, works out what it would raise, and starts one free. A player holding a code types it in.

Left: double-height yellow LAST MAN STANDING, a one-line offer, then a results feed typing four scores with red OUT counts and a blinking YOUR PICK line. Right: a page index whose entries link to every section. Beneath: the giant ticking STILL STANDING count (SAMPLE), a yellow block "play free" action and a cyan "start one for your club" action, side by side. The fastext bar is fixed at the bottom.
