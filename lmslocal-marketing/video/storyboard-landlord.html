An earlier draft left matches-and-results out because the service is technically opt-in and priced, and that was too cautious: it is given to every new organiser as part of the package, so for anyone watching this it is simply true. The one thing to watch is shelf life, not honesty — the day it stops being included, this scene needs recutting, exactly as the printed artwork carrying "free right now" does.

Open question: whether this card carries a tracking path the way the leaflet's QR does (/bar-a). A plain lmslocal.co.uk is typed more reliably but produces no number, and the leaflet's whole measurement argument says the number matters.

Notes for the render

  • 25s total, 30fps, 750 frames. Motion scenes render through Chrome's virtual time budget the way make-png.js already drives it, so frame 200 is always the same pixels and a re-render is reproducible.
  • Screen scenes are captured separately at 1920×1080 and cropped to square on the action — capturing at square loses the app's layout entirely.
  • Silent. No music in v1: a licensed track can be muxed in later with ffmpeg without touching the picture.
  • Facebook's feed caps usefully at about 30s for this kind of post, so 25s leaves headroom and nothing needs cutting if a scene runs long in the edit.
