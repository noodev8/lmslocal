# Higgsfield — notes for the cartoon promo

**Status: nothing bought.** The brief is `../ai-brief/cartoon-promo.md`.

- **Plan:** subscribe for one month, make the film, pull everything down (the trained character
  above all), cancel. Plus ~$59/mo, Ultra ~$129/mo as of 2026-09-12 (mid-promotion — recheck).
  Starter's 270 credits won't make a film.
- **Open question before buying:** does character consistency come from the `soul` training tool
  (free generations on Plus) or Nano Banana Pro (unlimited on Ultra only)? That decides the plan.
- **Unlimited only applies on the website.** The CLI (`npx skills add higgsfield-ai/skills`,
  `higgsfield auth login`) always spends credits.
- **Division of labour:** Andreas on the web (unlimited) for character training and stills;
  Claude on the CLI only for final shots; assembly, captions, dialogue and real screenshots done
  locally with ffmpeg. Don't burn credits on work that's free in the browser.
- **The real product:** hard-cut to real screenshots from `shots/`, never AI-rendered screens.
  Compositing into a generated phone works only on a static shot.
