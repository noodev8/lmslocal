# ai-brief

Briefs for artwork and film that an **outside AI tool** produces, because the pipelines in this
folder cannot. Not artwork itself — nothing here renders. These are the documents that get written
*before* a subscription is bought, so day one of a paid month is spent generating rather than
deciding what to generate.

| File | What it briefs |
|---|---|
| `cartoon-promo.md` | The 65s cartoon promo. Story, dialogue, characters, shot list. |

The tool this was written against is Higgsfield — see `../video/higgsfield.md` for the plan
economics, the Plus-vs-Ultra question and the division of labour between Andreas on the web UI
(free at the margin) and Claude on the CLI (metered). **Read that first**; it decides what gets
generated where, and the one way to waste the month is to let Claude burn credits on the part
that is free in a browser.

## The rule that produced this folder

**Claude cannot draw.** Hand-written SVG and CSS give typography, geometry and motion — that is
`../video/film.html`, and it costs nothing. They do not give a landlord behind a bar, or a face.
So the test for anything briefed here is: *could this have been made in the existing pipeline?*
If yes, it does not belong in this folder, and paying for it is waste.

## Rules that carry over from the print and web work

These are not restated in each brief. They are not style preferences — most of them are there
because breaking one makes a claim we cannot stand behind.

- **Every "you" is the organiser.** Players are always third person.
- **"Matches", never "fixtures"** in anything a customer sees.
- **No invented numbers, no invented testimonials, no countdown urgency.** Every figure must
  already appear on the live site.
- **Never depict money changing hands** — no cash, no pot, no envelope, no amount chalked up.
  Depicting the prize reads as us operating the scheme (terms §6). This one is worth watching
  for actively, because a generated pub scene will happily put a pile of notes on the bar and
  the shot is then unusable.
- **Anything opt-in is never stated as universal.** The fixture service is currently given to
  every new organiser, which is why the film claims it; the day that changes, every asset
  carrying it needs recutting.
