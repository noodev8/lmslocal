# ai-brief

Briefs for artwork an **outside AI tool** has to make, because the pipelines here can't draw
people. Nothing in this folder renders. If something could be built in HTML/CSS
(`../video/film.html`), it doesn't belong here.

| File | What it briefs |
|---|---|
| `cartoon-promo.md` | The 65s cartoon promo: story, dialogue, characters, shot list |

Tool notes: `../video/higgsfield.md`.

Rules every brief inherits:

- "You" is always the organiser; players are third person. "Matches", never "fixtures".
- No invented numbers or testimonials, no countdown urgency.
- **Never show money changing hands** — no cash, pot or amount. It reads as us running the
  scheme (terms §6). Generated pub scenes add a pile of notes on the bar unprompted; watch for it.
- Nothing opt-in stated as universal.
- An image model can't draw a scannable QR — leave a square and composite `../assets/site-qr.png`.
